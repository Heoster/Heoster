## 🚀 About Me

I'm **Harsh Saini**, professionally known as **Heoster** — a passionate **Full-Stack Engineer**, student, and founder from **Khatauli (Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh), India**.

I specialize in building innovative AI-powered applications and seamless web experiences. I love combining cutting-edge AI technology with beautiful, responsive design to create solutions that make a real impact.

Currently studying **Class 12 PCM** at **Maples Academy Khatauli**, and founder of **[CODEEX-AI](https://codeex-ai.vercel.app)**. Driven by the belief that advanced AI tools should be accessible to everyone at **zero cost**, I focus on building open-source platforms, intelligent auto-routers, and full-stack developer applications that serve users worldwide.

Always learning. Always building. 🔥

> *Intelligence should be free, fast, and useful for everyone.*
